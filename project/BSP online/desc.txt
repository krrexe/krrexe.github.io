BSP online is a doom engine inspired simple BSP/portal rendered deathmatch online multiplayer.
Have fun with friend.

Controls:
Menu: arrowkey up/down
Servers: Tab to choose, enter to join or host.

Classic controls:
Arrows up, down to move forward and backwards,
Arrows left, right to look left and right,
ALT+Arrows left,right to strafe left and right
1,2,3,4,5,6 to choose weapons
T to chat

Modern controls:
WASD to move, mouse to look, left click for shooting.

1,2,3,4,5,6 to choose weapons
T to chat

Weapons:
Pistol: Deals 10 damage
Shotgun: Deals 20 damage
Rifle: Deals 5 damage
Minigun: Deals 2 damage
Double Barrel: Deals 35 damage
Rocket launcher: Deals 50 damage

tips:
constantly strafe to avoid getting shot, while doing that,
use rifle or rocket launcher to shoot your friend.

Servers:
while playing you can ban player by /ban <playername> in chat.
which will cause DATA/server/banned.txt will come out.
you can also put icon.bmp on DATA/server/ to choose icon for server in join menu.
if you and your friend shares same map1.map made in mapEditor.exe, 
then you can play in completely other map.
